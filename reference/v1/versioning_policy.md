# Model and Data Versioning Policy

## Dataset versioning

- Raw label snapshots are timestamped in `data/raw/*.meta.json`.
- Processed training snapshots are regenerated by `scripts/build_training_dataset.py`.
- Provenance document: `data/processed/training_dataset.provenance.json`.

## Model versioning

- Artifact path: `data/checkpoints/risk_model.joblib`.
- Metrics path: `data/checkpoints/risk_model.metrics.json`.
- Artifact includes:
  - `model_type`
  - `feature_names`
  - `trained_at`
  - `sample_count`
  - key metrics

## Promotion gates

Promote only if all pass:
1. AUC and PR-AUC are non-degrading versus prior baseline.
2. Brier score does not worsen materially.
3. Explainability output still maps to top reason codes.
4. Policy hard-stops remain unchanged unless approved by risk governance.

## Rollback

- Keep previous artifact copy before replacement.
- Re-point `MODEL_ARTIFACT_PATH` to prior artifact and restart API.
